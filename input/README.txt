Put your three files here, then run:  python reconcile.py

  orders.csv      one row per order (or per product line of an order)
  payments.csv    the card payments from your payment processor
  columns.xlsx    says which of your columns is which

orders and payments can also be .tsv or .xlsx.
For columns.xlsx, copy columns_example.xlsx from the main folder into this
folder, rename it, and write your own column names in the yellow cells.

The files stay on your computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
